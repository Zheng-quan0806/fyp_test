Notebook Tutor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Notebook Tutor handles information when a user accesses the application. Notebook Tutor is an educational Final Year Project and does not sell personal information or display targeted advertisements.

Information processed

When Google sign-in is used, the application may receive the user's name, email address, profile picture, and an authentication identifier. The application may also process content supplied by the user, including notes, drawings, uploaded files, AI prompts, AI conversations, community messages, calendar tasks, and study-progress information.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide application features, authenticate the user, synchronize their data, generate requested AI responses, support community communication, schedule reminders, and maintain study progress.

Storage and service providers

Some information is stored locally on the user's device. For signed-in users, Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage, and backend functions. Google provides account authentication. User prompts and attached content submitted to the AI feature may be processed by the configured Gemini service to produce a response.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared with service providers only when required to operate the requested features. Community content is shared with its intended chat participants. Data may remain available while the account or related content exists, subject to the application's deletion controls and the service providers' retention requirements.

User choices

Users may use supported guest features without Google sign-in, sign out of their account, and delete supported notes, conversations, messages, or tasks through the application. Data-removal questions can be submitted using the contact address below.

Security

Reasonable technical controls are used to protect cloud data, including authenticated access and Supabase Row Level Security policies. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact zhengquanchin@gmail.com.
